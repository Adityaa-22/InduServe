Find Approvals Explore Services

Why a single window

Starting and operating an industrial business often requires interaction with multiple departments, approval authorities and compliance systems. Businesses typically need to identify applicable licences, prepare separate applications for each department, track status across disconnected systems and keep up with recurring compliance deadlines.

This portal brings approval discovery, application submission, tracking, compliance management and scheme information into a single platform, so a business can manage its regulatory requirements from one place instead of several.

Government Services

Core services available through the portal.

Industrial Approvals

Search applicable approvals and licences by industry, state and department, with details on eligibility, documents and fees.

Application Tracking

Track the status of submitted applications against a clear, stage-by-stage timeline.

Compliance Management

View recurring compliance requirements, due dates and completion status across departments.

Government Schemes

Browse schemes, subsidies and incentives, and check indicative eligibility for your business.

Document Management

Upload and manage documents required across applications from a single account.

Notifications & Alerts

Receive updates on application status, upcoming deadlines and relevant scheme announcements.

Benefits

What a single-window approach aims to improve.

  • ✓ Single-window access to approvals across departments
  • ✓ Reduced manual, paper-based processes
  • ✓ Transparent, stage-wise application tracking
  • ✓ Centralised storage for application documents
  • ✓ Reminders for recurring compliance deadlines
  • ✓ Easier discovery of government schemes and incentives
